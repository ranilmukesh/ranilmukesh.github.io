This work treats 6G security as an active defense problem, where the network does not just detect malicious behavior but intentionally attracts, observes, and learns from attackers using adaptive decoy entities. Its main value is the integration of generative AI, RL-based decoy management, edge deployment, network slicing, and federated learning into one practical framework designed for low-latency, large-scale, and constantly changing wireless environments.
