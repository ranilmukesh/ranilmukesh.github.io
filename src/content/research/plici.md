abstract: "A probabilistic contrastive learning framework unifying vision and language representations through uncertainty-aware embeddings for robust cross-modal retrieval."
tags: ["Contrastive Learning", "Vision-Language", "Multimodal AI", "Probabilistic Models"]
---

PLICI introduces uncertainty-aware embeddings into the CLIP-style contrastive paradigm. By modelling each representation as a distribution rather than a point, the framework achieves more robust cross-modal alignment, especially under noisy or ambiguous inputs.
