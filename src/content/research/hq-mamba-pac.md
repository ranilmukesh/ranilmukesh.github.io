HQ-Mamba-PAC integrates quantum-inspired state-space layers into the Mamba selective SSM framework, coupled with a policy-aware compression module that prunes state transitions by learned importance scores. The result is sub-linear scaling on long-context benchmarks with minimal accuracy degradation.
